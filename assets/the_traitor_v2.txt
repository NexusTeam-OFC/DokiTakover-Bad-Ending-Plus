V2 DOPPELGÄNGER

IDENTIFICATION

Name: V2 Doppelgänger
Type: Flawed Impersonator
Origin: Old Faker
Status: Anomalous Entity
Primary Target: Monika
Primary Interest: BF
Narrative Role: Antagonist / Psychological Manipulator

---

ORIGIN

The V2 Doppelgänger is the evolved form of Old Faker, originally associated with The Traitor, currently part of GF Week.

Her evolution takes place specifically in The Traitor (Extended Mix), associated with Week "???", which will be revealed in V3.

During the Extended Mix, a flaw in GF's file allows the Eyes to interfere with the system. The entity exploits this vulnerability to achieve a more advanced form.

From this point onward, Old Faker becomes the V2 Doppelgänger.

The V2 Doppelgänger is not a separate entity, but the same entity in a new stage of existence.

Chronological distinction:

- The Traitor: The original Old Faker song, currently part of GF Week.
- The Traitor (Extended Mix): The song in which Old Faker evolves into the V2 Doppelgänger, associated with Week "???", to be revealed in V3.

The transformation does not occur in the original version of The Traitor. It takes place specifically in the Extended Mix.

---

PERSONALITY

The Doppelgänger is calculating, manipulative, and cruel. Her primary strategy is to undermine her victims' perception of reality before resorting to physical violence.

She observes behavior, identifies vulnerabilities, and waits for favorable opportunities to act. Her methods involve creating a false sense of security while manipulating her target.

Dominant Trait: Yandere.

Her primary interest is BF. The Doppelgänger mistakenly believes that Monika has romantic feelings for him and interprets Monika's concern for BF's safety as a threat.

In reality, Monika has no romantic interest in BF. Her concern is genuine and stems from her desire to protect his well-being.

This distorted interpretation makes Monika one of the entity's primary targets.

---

OBJECTIVES

Primary Objective

Psychologically destroy her victims.

In After Story, her primary target is Monika. The entity seeks to induce psychological collapse through mental manipulation, perceptual distortion, and the exploitation of emotional vulnerabilities.

Her methods do not rely exclusively on physical attacks. She prioritizes control over her victims' perceptions and mental states.

Exception: BF

BF occupies an exceptional position within the Doppelgänger's psychology.

Initially, she lacks genuine human emotions and uses emotional displays as instruments of manipulation.

However, her growing proximity to BF begins to trigger the development of real emotions.

This change distinguishes her relationship with BF from her relationships with other victims.

---

ABILITIES

1. Psychological Manipulation

Manipulates mental states, perceptions, and interpretations of reality. She can induce victims to believe false information or accept events that never occurred.

2. Illusions

Creates illusions that alter her victims' sensory perceptions. She can use them to confuse, disorient, intimidate, or manipulate.

3. Memory Manipulation

Interferes with existing memories and can implant false memories, making fictional events appear genuine.

4. Perceptual Manipulation

Controls the information her victims perceive and the conclusions they draw from it.

She does not necessarily need to lie directly to induce false beliefs.

5. Voice and Audio Mimicry

Reproduces human voices, music, radio transmissions, and other sounds.

This ability allows her to attract, confuse, or manipulate individuals without immediately revealing her location.

6. Shapeshifting

Assumes the appearance of other individuals, including acquaintances, family members, strangers, and specific people.

To perform an impersonation, she requires a physical item associated with the target, such as a lock of hair, a strand of hair, or a piece of clothing.

7. Flawed Impersonation

Despite her advanced mimicry, the Doppelgänger belongs to the Flawed Impersonator category.

Her transformations may exhibit anomalies, including:

- Facial deformities.
- Missing anatomical features.
- Abnormally elongated limbs.
- Bodily distortions.
- Vocal irregularities.
- Physical or behavioral inconsistencies.

Perfect impersonation is not guaranteed under all circumstances.

8. Anomalous Manifestation

Manifests suddenly in unexpected locations, including monitored environments.

9. Levitation

Possesses the ability to levitate, defying conventional human physical limitations.

10. Reality Manipulation

Interferes with the structure of reality and reconstructs events and cycles.

She can rebuild the game's CORE and alter fundamental components of its operation.

This ability has specific limitations.

11. File and System Manipulation

Interferes with the game's files and internal structures by exploiting existing vulnerabilities.

During the world's reconstruction, she removes "limitations.dta", creating an opening that allows "monika.chr" to enter.

This interference contributes to the events of Monika Week.

12. Psychological Destruction

Can drive victims into extreme states of psychological deterioration.

One possible outcome is M.A.D. (Metaphysical Awareness Disorder).

In Executable File, this process is associated with Sayori's psychological collapse and induced suicide.

13. Physical, Psychic, and Mental Abilities

Possesses anomalous physical attributes and psychic and mental capabilities, including telekinesis.

These abilities complement her psychological manipulation rather than necessarily serving as her primary method of attack.

---

LIMITATIONS

1. Psychological Dependence

The effectiveness of her psychological abilities decreases considerably when a victim can:

- Overcome her illusions.
- Refuse to listen to her.
- Maintain emotional control.
- Resist fear and manipulation.

The entity is most dangerous when she can control her target's perception and mental state.

2. Impersonation Failures

Her transformations are not infallible.

Her classification as a Flawed Impersonator means that physical, vocal, or behavioral inconsistencies may occur.

3. Physical Barriers

Despite her supernatural abilities, she cannot indiscriminately bypass every physical barrier.

Access to certain environments may still depend on available entry points, such as doors, windows, or other openings.

4. Initial Lack of Genuine Emotions

Originally, the Doppelgänger lacks authentic human emotions.

Her displays of love, empathy, concern, sadness, and affection are initially performances intended to manipulate others.

Exception: BF.

Her proximity to BF gradually triggers the development of genuine emotions, making him an anomaly within her psychological structure.

---

RELATIONSHIPS

GF

The V2 Doppelgänger kills the original GF and assumes her identity.

She uses this appearance throughout the Weeks to remain close to BF without raising suspicion.

Despite her physical resemblance to GF, the impersonation may reveal inconsistencies:

- Behavior inconsistent with the original GF.
- Vocal irregularities.
- Emotional differences.
- Physical discrepancies.
- Memory inconsistencies.
- The absence of GF's bracelet.

BF

BF is her primary object of interest.

The Doppelgänger intends to keep him close and establish control over their relationship.

However, this proximity produces an unexpected change: the entity begins developing genuine feelings.

Her central emotional contradiction is that she develops real emotions while continuing to use manipulation as a means of establishing intimacy.

Monika

The Doppelgänger considers Monika a rival because she misinterprets the relationship between Monika and BF.

Monika wants to protect BF, not pursue a romantic relationship with him.

The entity distorts this concern and attempts to psychologically destroy Monika.

In Conscience, she assumes Sayori's appearance to exploit the emotional importance Sayori holds for Monika.

Sayori

The Doppelgänger uses Sayori's identity as an instrument of emotional manipulation against Monika.

In Executable File, this strategy culminates in extreme psychological deterioration associated with M.A.D.

The Player

The Doppelgänger manipulates both BF and the player.

Throughout the Weeks, the player believes they control BF and experience events through their decisions.

The distinction between the two versions of The Traitor is essential to the entity's chronology:

- The Traitor: The original Old Faker song, currently part of GF Week.
- The Traitor (Extended Mix): The version in which Old Faker evolves into the V2 Doppelgänger, associated with Week "???", which will be revealed in V3.

The Extended Mix marks the entity's evolution and establishes a new stage of her existence.

The Doppelgänger manipulates the information revealed to the player, influencing what they believe is happening.

---

CORE TRAIT

The defining characteristic of the V2 Doppelgänger is her use of identity, perception, and trust as instruments of manipulation.

Her effectiveness does not depend exclusively on physical strength or supernatural superiority, but on her ability to assume trusted identities and undermine her victims' judgment.

The V2 Doppelgänger is an anomalous entity classified as a Flawed Impersonator, originating from Old Faker's evolution during The Traitor (Extended Mix).

Primary Objective: Psychological destruction of her victims, particularly Monika.

Primary Interest: BF, whose proximity triggers the development of genuine emotions within the entity.
